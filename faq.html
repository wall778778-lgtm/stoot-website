FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about STOOT.

What is STOOT?

STOOT is an independent space shooter game focused on combat, survival and asteroid destruction. Players control a spacecraft, fight enemies and try to survive increasingly challenging battles.

Is STOOT free to play?

Yes. STOOT is available as a free independent game project. Players can download and play the current version without requiring a purchase.

What platforms does STOOT support?

Currently, STOOT supports Windows PC. Future versions may include improvements and additional platform support.

Does STOOT require an internet connection?

No. The game is designed to run offline after downloading. An internet connection is only needed for downloading the game and visiting the website.

What technology was used to create STOOT?

STOOT was developed using C++ and SFML. The project was created from scratch as an independent game development project.

Will STOOT receive future updates?

Development is continuing. Future updates may include new enemies, gameplay systems, improvements and additional content.

Where can I contact the developer?

You can contact the STOOT developer through the Contact page available on this website.
