ABOUT THE GAME

STOOT is an independent space shooter focused on fast combat, survival and asteroid destruction.

Take control of your spacecraft, destroy dangerous space rocks, fight enemies and collect powerful abilities while trying to survive the battlefield.

Large asteroids can split into smaller pieces, creating new threats around your ship. Stay mobile, watch your surroundings and choose your attacks carefully.

STOOT 3.0 is the current version of the project, with gameplay systems and improvements developed from scratch using C++ and SFML.
